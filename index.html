This tool 2026

This tool's five dimensions — identity, expression, anatomical sex, romantic attraction, and sexual attraction — aren't invented here. They come from two frameworks that reshaped how gender is taught. The Genderbread Person v4 broke gender into these same categories as independent scales, instead of one line running from "man" to "woman." The Gender Unicorn, from Trans Student Educational Resources, refined that into the near-identical five-part structure used here, splitting attraction into romantic and sexual. What changed on this end: instead of static bars, each dimension is a density field — because most people aren't a single point, they're a range, sometimes several peaks at once.
